# CYZOR V4 — Discovery

## Status
COMPLETE — approved as the structural source for the next phase.

## 1. Product definition

CYZOR is a modular business operating platform designed to connect company operations, organizational context, data and AI in one operating layer.

Core relationship:

Organization → Modules → Data → Context → Specialized Agents → CYZOR AI Manager → Intelligence → Decision → Action

The landing must communicate that CYZOR is not merely a collection of SaaS tools and not merely an AI chatbot. Its value comes from the relationship between operational software, shared context and an AI workforce.

## 2. Product domains

The platform can include:
- CRM
- ERP
- Projects
- People
- Documents
- BI
- Automations
- Finance
- Products
- Stock
- POS
- Agenda
- AI Workforce
- CYZOR AI Manager

The landing should expose the breadth of the platform without becoming a catalog of every module.

## 3. Primary narrative

The visitor should understand the product in this order:

1. CYZOR gives the company one operating environment.
2. Business modules connect instead of operating as isolated applications.
3. Operational data becomes shared context.
4. Specialized AI agents understand individual domains.
5. CYZOR AI Manager consolidates those signals.
6. Intelligence becomes decisions and actions.
7. The visitor can see evidence of the actual product interface.

## 4. Landing objective

The landing should answer within the first visual sequence:
- What is CYZOR?
- Who is it for?
- Why is the architecture different?
- What does the product actually look like?
- How does AI participate?
- What is the next action?

The page should prioritize product comprehension over generic marketing claims.

## 5. Audience

The visual language must support both physical and digital businesses. It should feel appropriate to an owner, executive, operator or technology-oriented decision maker without assuming a specific industry.

Do not create industry-specific promises unless supported by actual product capabilities.

## 6. Visual proof hierarchy

The strongest proof should be visual and structural:
- product interface;
- connected module architecture;
- AI Workforce / specialized agents;
- data-to-decision flow;
- operational states;
- infrastructure/security representation.

Avoid unsupported testimonials, customer logos, adoption metrics, revenue numbers, performance claims, certifications or fabricated case studies.

## 7. HTML versus visual assets

### HTML owns
- navigation;
- headlines;
- supporting copy;
- CTAs;
- labels;
- navigation links;
- important product information;
- cards;
- semantic structure;
- accessible states.

### Assets own
- complex neural/system visualizations;
- abstract infrastructure structures;
- AI agent networks;
- product/environment illustrations;
- complex visual flows;
- visual atmosphere.

Generated images must never be used to hide important text that should remain searchable, accessible and responsive HTML.

## 8. Interaction requirements

The landing should support:
- clear primary CTA;
- secondary exploration/demo CTA;
- navigation;
- hover/focus states;
- restrained product visual motion;
- signal propagation / system activation where meaningful;
- responsive recomposition.

Motion must communicate system behavior, not exist merely as decoration.

## 9. Visual direction

The direction is modern neural technology:
- dark-first;
- high contrast;
- CYZOR lime as signal/accent;
- graphite/black structural surfaces;
- precise typography;
- technical metadata;
- neural nodes;
- traces;
- system maps;
- layered product surfaces;
- controlled depth.

Avoid:
- generic SaaS templates;
- generic AI landing pages;
- purple/blue AI language;
- crypto/Web3 aesthetics;
- cyberpunk/gaming styling;
- excessive neon;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- decorative grids without meaning;
- giant isolated AI brains/robots;
- fake dashboards;
- poster or banner composition.

## 10. Content architecture

The structural narrative should be approximately:

Navigation
→ Hero / CYZOR identity
→ Connected operating system explanation
→ Neural/AI Workforce architecture
→ Module ecosystem
→ Data → Context → Intelligence → Decision → Action
→ Product interface evidence
→ Infrastructure / security
→ Decision/action surface
→ Final activation CTA
→ Footer

The exact number and proportions of sections must be determined by the wireframe rather than forcing content into a predefined template.

## 11. Responsive principle

Desktop should use spatial composition and layered relationships.

Tablet should reduce density while preserving hierarchy.

Mobile should become a deliberate vertical sequence:
message → proof → system → product → AI → action.

Do not simply scale desktop down.

## 12. Structural success criteria

The wireframe must:
- clearly distinguish every section;
- establish exact hierarchy and approximate dimensions;
- reserve visual asset areas before asset generation;
- distinguish HTML content from future visual assets;
- define desktop and mobile behavior;
- prevent later improvisation of asset placement.

## 13. Visual generation policy

Discovery produces no images.

The next phase produces exactly one low-fidelity wireframe image. It must be a structural artifact, not a polished design or visual concept.

No additional visual exploration is permitted before the final composition checkpoint.
